🦈 Hawaiʻi Shark Incidents

An interactive look at documented shark-bite incidents across the Hawaiian Islands, built from the official State of Hawaiʻi DLNR incident list. Filter by time range, island, activity, and outcome.

⚠️ Please read before drawing conclusions from this map. This dashboard visualizes historical shark-bite reports for research and educational purposes only — it is not a safety guide or risk rating for any beach, break, or island. Shark bites are extremely rare relative to the number of people in Hawaiian waters every day, and a place with markers simply has documented history, not necessarily elevated present-day risk; a place with none may simply be less visited or have shorter reporting history. Please don't use this data as your sole basis for choosing, avoiding, or favoring a location. Always check current conditions, posted signage, and local ocean-safety advisories before entering the water, and follow guidance from lifeguards and local authorities. Be respectful of the ocean, of sharks and other marine wildlife (native and ecologically important), of reefs, and of local communities and their relationship with these places. Locations shown are approximate (place-name based, not exact GPS). This project and its author accept no liability for decisions made, or outcomes resulting from, use of this information — you assume full responsibility for your own ocean safety choices. Mahalo!
